## Numbered findings and fixes

1. LOW, R-19/R-31: binary header hiding did not satisfy gradual scroll motion. The shared header now moves upward in proportion to travel from 30px to the midpoint of the second section, returns over 160px upward travel, and fades to flat charcoal. Removed the white top highlight as requested. Keyboard focus and an open mobile menu hold it visible.
2. LOW, R-31: contact typography and field heights were oversized. Reduced the shared form's heading, body, labels, inputs and message box; preserved readable mobile fonts and at least 44px controls. The separate Contact page follows its supplied full-width reference.
3. MEDIUM, R-19/R-26: resetting the gallery directly from the end to scroll position zero produced a visible rewind. Added repeated photo cycles and reset to identical frames after settling. A recorded normal-motion check confirms a complete forward frame movement before the invisible cycle reset. Side images scale to 85%; center images remain full size.
4. LOW, R-23/R-31: the old footer image was too small to remain sharp at the requested size. Replaced it with the exact higher-resolution PNG from the user's logo folder. CSS trims its transparent vertical margins; the source file is unchanged.
5. HIGH, R-26/R-27: an email-app review flow did not fulfill the new FormSubmit instruction. All four forms now POST to the specified AJAX endpoint with complete contact details. Pending disables controls; success resets the form; rejection retains values and exposes retry plus email/phone alternatives. A 20-second timeout prevents indefinite pending state. Browser tests intercept requests; no test emails were sent.
6. HIGH, R-26/R-32: a short native country select did not match the requested searchable flag list. Added 245 country entries, local licensed SVG flags, name/code/dialing-code search, accent-insensitive matching, empty feedback, arrow navigation, Enter selection, Escape dismissal, outside dismissal and focus restoration. Dropdown width is constrained to the viewport.
7. LOW, R-19/R-31: the requested Home section layering required opaque incoming surfaces. Scoped sticky pairs now pin the hero and testimonials while their following sections cover them. Their opaque backgrounds prevent earlier text showing through fading photos. Taller pinned sections use a negative top offset to expose their bottom content.
8. HIGH, R-32: reveal animations could momentarily hide a keyboard-focused control. Focus within a reveal immediately restores opacity and removes its transition. Reduced motion also shows all content immediately and disables section overlap.
9. HIGH, R-25: the earlier orange on light gray measured 3.92:1 after contact text was made smaller. Updated light-surface orange links to #b34300, measured at 4.74:1. Country-search placeholder contrast is 5.28:1; country-option text is 10.58:1.
10. LOW, R-24: Services category buttons still used local form anchors after the new Contact page was requested. All nine now open `/contact`; each destination is clicked and verified in the Services browser check.
11. LOW, R-19/R-31: the user refined Home hero text to enter from left to right. Wordmark and intro now begin 40px left of their final position and use 650ms ease-in timing; other section reveals remain vertical.
12. HIGH, R-24: directly loading a hash URL could precede React's section render. Initial hash scrolling now runs after the section exists, retaining the intended anchors and reserved navbar space.

All findings are resolved within authorized scope. The reference palette, real business copy, supplied photographs and established page structure remain the design direction. No fabricated assets or marketing claims were added.
